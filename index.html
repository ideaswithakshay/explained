Level 2 · P/E ratio

A lemonade stand costs $500 and earns $50 a year. At that pace it takes 10 years to earn back the price. That number, price ÷ profit, is the P/E ratio.

Level 8 · P/E ratio

A P/E of 28 means an earnings yield of 1 ÷ 28 = 3.6%. If a safe government bond pays more than that, the price only makes sense if profit grows. A high P/E is a bet on growth.

Can I learn from my own links?

Yes. Paste any article, video or paper. Explained breaks it into short steps at your level, explains every term, and connects each idea to the rest of your map.

When you pick a topic, a 10-question quiz places you from 1 (total beginner) to 10 (expert). Every slide is written in 10 versions, so you start where you are and move up as you get things right. You can change your level anytime.

How deep does it go?

As deep as you want. Every idea has a step under it that explains it. Swipe up to open the next step. Swipe back to return to the story.

Where do the numbers come from?

Official data, court rulings, scientific papers, company filings and news reports. Every fact card shows its source. Green tags mean a filing or official number. Gold tags mean a news report.

Who is Explained for?

Anyone curious who feels lost when the jargon starts, from total beginners to people who work in the field. Nothing is assumed. If a word stops you, swipe up and it gets explained, all the way down.

Does it take sides?

No. When people disagree, you see each side in its own words, with the same space for both. Explained never tells you how to vote, or what to buy or sell. You decide.
