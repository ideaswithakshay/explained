Privacy Policy

Explained was called Finspeak until November 2026. This page covers the app under either name.

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Explained explains the news, and any link you paste, step by step in short swipe cards. This page covers the Explained app and this website.

The app

You don't need an account

You can read every free slide without signing in. Your progress is saved on your phone: the stories you've read, the ideas on your map, your review schedule and the slides you've opened.

Sign in with Apple (optional)

If you tap Sign in with Apple, Apple gives us a random user number for you. We ask Apple for no name and no email. We then save a copy of your progress under that number, so it follows you to a new phone. The copy includes the stories you've read, your review schedule, the slides you've opened, and the links you've had explained. It is stored with Cloudflare, on servers that may be outside your country.

Links you paste

When you paste a link, our server reads that page and sends its text to Anthropic's AI (Claude) to write the explanation. We keep the explanation for 30 days, filed under the link, so the next person who pastes the same link gets it instantly. This copy is not tied to you. Don't paste links to anything private.

Daily link limit

To keep the free limit of 5 links a day fair, our server counts links by internet address (IP address). Each count is deleted after 2 days.

Subscriptions

Explained Plus is sold through Apple. We never see your card or your name. We use RevenueCat to check with Apple whether your plan is active. RevenueCat gets a random app user number and your purchase history. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we don't do

We don't show ads. We don't sell your data. We don't track you across other apps or websites. The app has no analytics tools.

Deleting your data

In the app, open the Review tab and tap Delete account. That deletes your account and the copy of your progress on our server right away. To erase the progress on your phone too, delete the app.

Who runs Explained

Explained is made by Akshay Ramachandran, an independent developer in the United States. He decides what data the app collects and why. To ask anything about your data, email us.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email us and we'll do it within 30 days. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the privacy regulator where you live: the ICO in the UK, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner in Canada or New Zealand, or the OAIC in Australia.

How long we keep it

We keep your saved progress until you delete your account. Explanations of pasted links are kept 30 days. Link counts are kept 2 days.

Links to other sites

Each story lists its sources, such as news sites and government pages. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

What Apple may share with us

If you chose to share data with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may send us crash reports and usage counts, like how many people opened the app. These don't include your name.

Children

Explained is not meant for children under 13.

The earlier word-lessons app

An earlier version of Explained, called Finspeak, taught finance words. It saved progress with Google Firebase and counted lesson use with PostHog, tied to a random account number. If you used it, email us and we'll delete that data.

This website

What we keep

If you join the waitlist, we store your email, the level you picked, and which phone you use. We also count page visits and lesson taps, tied to a random visitor number, not your name.

What we use it for

Only to tell you about Explained and to learn which levels people want most. We don't sell it or share it.

Links you paste

If you paste a link on this website, we save the link with your visit so we can see which topics people want explained. To explain it, we read the page and send its text to Anthropic’s AI (Claude), and we save the explanation so the next person who pastes the same link gets it instantly. Don’t paste links to anything private.

Deleting it

Reply to any Explained email and ask, and we'll delete your details.

Not advice

Explained teaches what the news and its words mean. It is not investment advice.

Questions

Email us, or see the support page.
