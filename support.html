Support

Explained was called Finspeak until November 2026. This page covers the app under either name.

Stuck, or found a mistake in a lesson? Email us. We usually reply within a day.

How do reviews work?

Key facts from the stories you read come back as short questions in the Review tab, just before you're likely to forget them. Get one right and it comes back later. Get it wrong and it comes back sooner.

How does Explained Plus work?

You get 10 free slides a day. Explained Plus opens every slide of every story, for $4.99 a month or $39.99 a year. Links you paste are capped at 5 a day for everyone. To cancel, open your iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. To get a plan back on a new phone, tap Restore purchases on the Plus screen.

How do I delete my account?

Open the Review tab and tap Delete account, then tap again to confirm. This deletes your account and everything saved on our server.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

Sign in with Apple in the Review tab, and your progress is saved to your account. Sign in on the new phone and it comes back. Without signing in, progress stays on the phone.

Where do the facts come from?

From official data, company filings, court opinions, science papers and news reports. Each story ends with its sources, and each fact is marked Confirmed, Likely or Unsure.

Is this investment advice?

No. Explained teaches what the news means. It doesn't tell you what to buy, sell or vote for.

Privacy

The app keeps as little as it can, with no ads and no tracking. For exactly what, see the privacy policy.
